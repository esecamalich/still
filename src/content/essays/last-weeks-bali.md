The last few weeks in Bali, a renewed energy towards my photography has risen. A sense of urgency, for who knows when I'll be back, and I better be sure that my job here is done before it's too late.

It's hard to define an ending to projects that could easily span the whole universe if you let them run wild. Same as it is hard to define when your time in a place is over.

"You'll know when it's done", I've been told, and though I know in my core this to be true, it's hard for me to truly believe it. The expectation is sometimes to hard to handle.

My mind likes clean cut, well defined boundaries, but life has proven to me that it's nothing but a convoluted mesh of memories blurred by time, gathered like dust in the corners of my mind.

I'm looking forward to being out of here, yet I'm scared. Funny how two opposite realities can be true at the same time.

This expectation fo mine, for things to fit neatly into their compartments, ignores some of my most important lessons.

So, yes, the work will be done once it's done.

Time and time again, I muse remember:

> My reward is not in the finishing, but in the doing.
