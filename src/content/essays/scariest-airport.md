The thing they don't tell you when you first buy your plan ticket to the Azores
is that not everybody can land in here. It takes a certain kind of human
to not succumb under the pressure of landing an aircraft through crosswinds
with the weight of a few dozen souls above their shoulders. 
Trying myself not to cry nor run into a panic, I had to give it to the pilot ––
he was on a different level!

The plane tossed and turned all the way down but it never gave up to the turbulence. 
As soon we stopped, a group of local school kids traveling along with us 
burst into cheers and nervous laughs. Everybody on board was tripping on adrenaline.

Once we were safe on the ground, whispers of the pilot's name began circulating. 
"Today's Filipe", some people exclaimed knowingly. 

Of course, he was one of the best ones.
