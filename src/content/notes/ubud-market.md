Six years after I first began making my way through Bali with a camera, I return to the place where it all started: 

Downtown Ubud.

I begin with a prayer, hoping for the closure of this chapter of my life to do justice to this place and the learnings it brought upon me.

From here, we move onto the downtown's main intersection, where the traffic struggles to move across its unaligned corners.

If we walk a few hundred meters west over Jalan Raya, we'll find a Starbucks, outside of which barefoot workers with no protective gear rest during a renovation project.

This is not an uncommon sight. All over Bali, teams of workers from faraway island are regularly seen been carried in the back of trucks, sleeping in plywood shacks by the construction sites. 

On the opposite direction lies Ubud's Art Market –– a recently rebuilt structure where sellers from across the island come to offer an assortment of trinkets and souvenirs.
