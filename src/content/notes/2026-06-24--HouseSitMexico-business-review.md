# What makes a business sellable?

Generally:

1. Predictable revenue

You already have some positive signals:

700+ paying members
Recurring renewals
10 years of operating history
Established brand
Word-of-mouth referrals

These are assets.

2. Systems instead of founder dependence

This is where I'd pay attention.

You already found:

Alex personally promotes listings in Facebook groups.
Alex seems heavily involved in operations.
Alex appears to be the public face of the business.

A buyer is often asking:

If Alexandra disappears tomorrow, does the business continue operating?

The more the answer is "yes," the more valuable the business becomes.

3. Clear acquisition channels

If she says:

Most members come from word of mouth and Google.

That's good.

If she can prove it with analytics, even better.

If growth depends entirely on Alex posting in Facebook groups, that's less attractive.

4. Documentation and processes

A future buyer wants:

documented workflows
onboarding procedures
customer support processes
marketing processes

You don't need to audit all of that now, but it's worth noticing.
