For my phase of Strange Visitations, I've decided to spend my time documenting Downtown Ubud.

After having made my way far and wide around the island for the past six years, focusing my efforts in such a small area is the best way to shine a light on the topics I've been studying during the past years.

Ubud being one of the main touristic centers in Bali, what was once known as a sleepy artist village is now a bustling destination for spiritual seekers, nomadic hustlers and those looking an escape from the coastal hubs.

During the short period of time I've lived in Ubud, I've seen the downtown area get back it's full energy, from the deserted era of the COVID pandemic to the post-lockdown hunger with which tourism came back.
