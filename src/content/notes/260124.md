# The Stars, The Stars

In the wake of intergalatic travel, humanity had no option but to get ready for what was to come.

By then, it was obvious that the old powers that be could no longer sustain the necessary efforts that this next phase in history demanded. Religions held no more control over people's beliefs, Governments held no more control over people's lifes –– and those who did would soon find out the sharp edge of the masses' sword.
