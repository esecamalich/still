I think we need to prioritize onboarding process, almost above everything else. But there’s a catch…

Alex has spoken about the importance of finding a good host/sitter match through right vibes, first impressions, and other such qualities. She talks about “the most important 5 minutes you could spend when house sitting”: a video call between prospect host(s) and sitter(s).

Here’s the thing, though: how much time do hosts actually have to video call with each potential sitter. Similarly: how much time does an interested sitter must spend when applying to multiple listings?

Is saving time for our users a priority? Getting them to the goal in as little steps as possible is, however you look at it.

In any case, the onboarding process shouldn’t stop at the moment a user creates an account. It should be for them to get their first meaningful interaction as early in their journey as possible.
