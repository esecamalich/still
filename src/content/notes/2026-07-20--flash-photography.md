Most of my life as a photographer, I've mostly shot natural light, but not necessarily sunlight only. What I mean is: light that was already there when I found it. In the corner of a dusty room, the glare reflected over a window, or the electric buzz of a tungsten lamp under a food cart.

If you're careful in noticing it, you'll learn there are infinite lights to be seen. At different speeds and different apertures.

I've loved it so much, so far I've had very little interest in learning how to use tools such as flashes and spotlights, beyond the basics of it. The self-imposed limitation led me to study light at all times, from dawn 'til dusk and in between. Reject the notions of good versus bad light, do's and dont's, and whatever other rules I had been told about photography.
